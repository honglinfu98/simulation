\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:background}

\paragraph{Marked temporal point processes.}
An MTPP models a sequence $\mathcal{H}_T=\{(t_i,e_i)\}_{i=1}^{N_T}$ of events
with times $t_i$ and types $e_i\in\mathcal{E}$, a finite set of atomic event
types, through per-type conditional intensities $\lambda_e(t\mid\mathcal{H}_t)$. With total intensity $\lambda(t)=\sum_{e}\lambda_e(t)$ and mark
distribution $p(e\mid t)=\lambda_e(t)/\lambda(t)$, the log-likelihood is
\[
\log\mathcal{L}
=\sum_i \Bigl[\log\lambda(t_i)+\log p(e_i\mid t_i)\Bigr]
-\int_0^T\!\lambda(u\mid\mathcal{H}_u)\,du ,
\]
the integral being the compensator
$\Lambda(t)=\int_0^t\lambda(u\mid\mathcal{H}_u)\,du$. Sampling uses Ogata
thinning \citep{lewis1979thinning,ogata1981lewis}, which requires a
dominating rate $\bar\lambda\ge\lambda(t)$; models exposing no practical
dominating rate fall back on heuristic bound estimates that can bias
samples. The \sppp{} authors avoid thinning for this very reason: its
dominating-rate and sampling-boundary hyperparameters ``can exacerbate
bias'' \citep{chang2025ctssm}. \ssppp's exact closed-form ceiling removes
the objection.

\paragraph{The \sppp{} backbone.}
\sppp{} \citep{chang2025ctssm} is a state-space point process: $L$ stacked
latent-linear-Hawkes/SSM layers with diagonal state, ZOH-discretized between
events and computable by parallel scan,
\[
x^{(\ell)}_{t'}=\bar A^{(\ell)} x^{(\ell)}_{t}
+(\bar A^{(\ell)}\!-\!I)\tilde B^{(\ell)} h^{(\ell-1)}
\bigl[+\tilde E^{(\ell)}\alpha\ \text{at an event}\bigr],
\]
with a post-norm residual readout (LayerNorm \citep{ba2016layernorm} after
each layer's residual sum) producing the continuous-time hidden
representation $h_t:=h^{(L)}(t)\in\mathbb{R}^{H}$. The published decoder
reads \emph{per-type} intensities from $h_t$ through a linear projection
followed by a scaled softplus with a learnable per-type sharpness,
$\lambda_e=\mathrm{softplus}\bigl(\beta_e\,a_e\bigr)/\beta_e$ with
$a=Wh_t+b$. Each $\beta_e$ reshapes the curvature near zero, but the map is
asymptotically linear with unit slope, so the head carries no explicit
output ceiling.

Our experiments use a \emph{factorized, uncapped} benchmark variant of
this decoder: a scalar total rate
$\lambda=\mathrm{softplus}(w^{\!\top}h_t+b)$ on one half of $h_t$, a
soft-max mark head on the other, built on our real-valued reimplementation
of the backbone. The reimplementation keeps the published configuration's
block structure (backward-ZOH input drive, input-dependent dynamics,
post-norm GELU residual readout, event impulses, left-limit likelihood) and
deviates in four respects: real diagonal states with log-spaced timescale
initialization (vs.\ the complex DPLR spectrum); a learned skip projection
(vs.\ identity residual with diagonal feedthrough); dynamics gates
conditioned at each interval's end (vs.\ start); and between-event queries
holding inter-layer inputs at last-event values. Our variant
shares the published head's operative property, a softplus rate with no
explicit ceiling, though its head is factorized rather than per-type.
\ssppp{} keeps everything up to and including $h_t$ and changes only the
heads, so \ssppp{}--\sppp{} differences are attributable to the head pair.

\paragraph{Neural TPPs for event streams.}
Neural MTPPs learn history-dependent intensities with recurrent
\citep{du2016recurrent,Mei2017TheProcess}, attention-based
\citep{zhang2020self,zuo2020thp}, jump-diffusion \citep{zhang2024njdtpp}, and
continuous-time state-space \citep{chang2025ctssm} encoders
\citep[survey:][]{Shchur2021NeuralReview}. \citet{Shi2022StateSimulation}
specialize neural Hawkes models to LOB streams and evaluate both prediction
and simulation; the state-space line, however, is evaluated
teacher-forced only, with state-of-the-art held-out likelihood but no
roll-outs \citep{chang2025ctssm}.

\paragraph{Hawkes modeling of order flow.}
Parametric Hawkes processes are the classical LOB event model
\citep{Hawkes1971,Hawkes2018HawkesReview,Jain2024LimitProcess,jain2024review},
with state-dependent extensions
\citep{Morariu-Patrichi2022State-dependentModelling}: exact stationarity
certificates, limited expressiveness. \ssppp{} takes the complementary
route: full neural expressiveness with a closed-form rate bound restoring
non-explosiveness, the bound a modeling decision rather than a thinning
device, one-sided so the quiet regime is left undistorted.

\paragraph{Generative LOB simulators.}
Learned order-flow generators span GANs \citep{li2020stockgan,coletta2021lobgan,
coletta2022worldagent}, token-level autoregressive state-space models
\citep{nagy2023lobs5}, diffusion-guided agents \citep{huang2026diga}, and
foundation-model-scale transformers \citep{mars2025,sood2026tradefm}.
Agent-based simulators \citep{byrd2020abides,frey2023jaxlob} remain the RL
workhorse. These systems are scored on distributional realism: stylized facts
\citep{cont2001empirical,vyetrenko2020getreal} and the KS/Wasserstein suites
of LOB-Bench \citep{nagy2025lobbench}, whose authors report error
accumulation over long roll-outs. \ssppp{} differs on two counts: one
likelihood-based model is held to \emph{both} prediction and closed-loop
realism, with the simulated mean rate calibrated and checked by full-scale
verification, a closed-loop test the simulators above do not report.
