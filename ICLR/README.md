## Notes
- EV-GAN: Simulation of extreme events with ReLU neural networks appears in the ICLR 2023 virtual-site listing but is a JMLR 2022 paper (vol 23, no 150) with no OpenReview record; excluded. The file "2023 ICLR - EV-GAN ... .pdf" in this folder is a WRONG download (arXiv 2411.10034, EveGuard) and should be deleted.
- No accepted ICLR 2017-2022 papers matched order-book or financial-market modeling. "Generating Realistic Stock Market Order Streams" was rejected at ICLR 2019 (later AAAI 2020).
- Non-accepted ICLR submissions on LOB modeling seen during the search (not downloaded): LOB-Bench (ICLR 2025 submission), TradeFM (ICLR 2026 submission), Detecting Multilevel Manipulation from Limit Order Book (ICLR 2026, withdrawn/not accepted), LOBBen-TM (ICLR 2026 submission), A Benchmark Study for LOB Models (ICLR 2025 submission).
